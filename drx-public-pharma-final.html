About the Founder
Built on Trust,
Driven by Purpose
"Healthcare should never be a privilege. Every person in Tamkuhi Raj deserves genuine medicines, expert doctors, and honest care."

Drx Parvej Ahmad is the founder of Public Pharma and the Clinic — a qualified pharmacist (D-Pharma, B-Pharma) who identified the gap in accessible, trustworthy healthcare in the Tamkuhi Raj region of Kushinagar. Starting with a single pharmacy, he built a complete healthcare ecosystem combining a pharmacy, multi-specialist clinic, and surgical wholesale supply under the philosophy that quality healthcare must reach every household.

His deep community connections and commitment to genuine medicines have made Public Pharma a trusted name across Tamkuhi Raj, Padrauna, Hata, and the wider Kushinagar district. Under his leadership, the organisation has grown to serve thousands of patients annually while maintaining its founding promise: Your Health, Our Priority.

Our Policies
Transparent & Patient-First

💊 Medicine Supply

Medicines dispensed only with valid prescription for Schedule H & H1 drugs. OTC medicines available without prescription. All sourced from licensed distributors only.

🔁 Return & Exchange

Medicines once dispensed are non-returnable as per drug regulations. Damaged or wrongly supplied items exchangeable within 24 hours with valid bill.

📅 Appointment Policy

Appointments are WhatsApp-based, confirmed on first-come basis. Walk-ins welcome subject to availability. Emergencies given priority over scheduled slots.

🔬 Surgical Supply Terms

Wholesale surgical supply requires valid GST & trade licence. Minimum order quantity applies. Bulk order payment terms discussed case-by-case.

🔒 Privacy Policy

Patient information, prescriptions & health data shared via WhatsApp is strictly confidential. Used only for the requested service. Never shared with third parties.

⚖️ Medical Disclaimer

Information on this website is for awareness only. Always consult our qualified doctors before starting any medication. In emergencies, contact a hospital immediately.
