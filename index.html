Medicine supply

Schedule H and H1 drugs are dispensed only with a valid prescription. OTC medicines are available without one. All stock comes from licensed distributors.

Return and exchange

Dispensed medicines can’t be returned, as per drug regulations. Damaged or wrongly supplied items can be exchanged within 24 hours with a valid bill.

Appointments

Appointments are booked on WhatsApp and confirmed first come, first served. Walk-ins are welcome subject to availability. Emergencies get priority over scheduled slots.

Surgical supply terms

Wholesale supply needs a valid GST number and trade licence. A minimum order quantity applies, and payment terms for bulk orders are discussed case by case.

Privacy

Patient information, prescriptions and health data shared on WhatsApp are strictly confidential. We use them only for the service you asked for and never share them with third parties.
